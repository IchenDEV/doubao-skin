# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, welcoming, and technically constructive project.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and accept corrections gracefully.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and security disclosure boundaries.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and deliberate disruption.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, issues, releases, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, restrict participation, or ban repeated or severe violations.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled according to their context and severity.
